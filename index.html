If you need help sooner

  • Very unwell, or it feels like an emergency: call 999 or go to A&E.
  • Need advice today: call NHS 111 or go to 111.nhs.uk.
  • Struggling to cope: call Samaritans on 116 123, free, any time.

This tool helps you organise what you want to say. It doesn't give medical advice or diagnose anything, and it doesn't look at or judge your answers. Always talk to your GP or a qualified clinician about your symptoms.
